## Overview

This project was carried out at the **Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT) – Rehab Technologies**, Genoa, as part of my Master's thesis in Biomedical Engineering (Biomedical Instrumentation specialization) at Politecnico di Torino.

The work focused on the simulation-based design and biomechanical evaluation of a lightweight lower-limb exoskeleton for Parkinson's disease patients.

## Key Contributions

- **Digital Twin Development:** built a high-fidelity musculoskeletal model of the human-exoskeleton system using OpenSim, integrating hardware kinematics and compliant transmission mechanics
- **Predictive Simulation:** formulated and solved optimal control problems (NLP) using the OpenSim Moco toolkit to simulate human-machine interaction
- **Biomechanical Assessment:** quantified mechanical unloading, neuromotor adaptation, and metabolic relief provided by the device
