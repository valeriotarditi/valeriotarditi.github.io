I am a Biomedical Engineering graduate from Politecnico di Torino, specialized in Biomedical Instrumentation. My work sits at the intersection of biomechanics, musculoskeletal simulation, and wearable assistive device development.

## Current Research

I recently completed my Master's Degree in Biomedical Engineering with a thesis work developed as Guest Researcher at the **Istituto Italiano di Tecnologia (IIT) – Rehab Technologies** in Genoa, focusing on the simulation-based design and biomechanical evaluation of a novel lower-limb exoskeleton for Parkinson's disease patients. This involved building digital twins in OpenSim and solving optimal control problems to simulate human-machine interaction.

## Background

I completed my Bachelor's Degree in Biomedical Engineering at Politecnico di Torino, where my final project focused on applying BM3D and CLAHE denoising algorithms to fluorescence microscopy images for GAN training.

- Wearable assistive devices and exoskeletons
- Biomedical Instrumentation
